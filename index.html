Use today

Create and edit follow-ups, copy messages, track status, pin tasks, and export backups. Nexus accounts support sign-in and recovery.

Workspace notes remain in your browser. Enable browser saving or export a backup to keep them. Follow-up dates do not trigger reminders.

Still being built

Telegram, X, and Discord authorization require provider setup. Account identity alone does not connect an inbox. AI drafting is currently available only in the local preview.
