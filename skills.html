Start with one task

  1. Download a skill into your assistant’s supported skills directory, using its skill name as the folder name.
  2. Connect your CRM through that assistant, or give it an export to review.
  3. Ask for a specific outcome: “Use crm-follow-up to draft a reply for this contact. Do not send it.”

These are assistant instructions, not native Nexus CRM integrations. Installing a skill does not connect an account or grant permissions. With an export, the assistant prepares proposals; it cannot update your CRM.
